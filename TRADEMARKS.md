# Trademarks

The `topic.earth` name, domain, logo, visual identity, interface identity, and
related project marks are reserved by the project steward.

The EUPL source-code licence does not grant permission to use the project's
trade names, trademarks, service marks, domain names, or visual identity except
as required for reasonable attribution and origin notices.

## Permitted Referential Use

You may use the `topic.earth` name to:

- describe the original source of the software;
- link to the upstream project;
- state that your work is based on or compatible with topic.earth;
- preserve required copyright, attribution, and licence notices.

## Not Permitted Without Permission

You may not use the `topic.earth` name, domain, logo, or visual identity to:

- imply endorsement by the project steward or future ASBL;
- operate a public fork that looks like the official topic.earth service;
- register confusingly similar domains, marks, social accounts, or app names;
- sell or promote unrelated products as official topic.earth products.

Public forks and deployments should use their own name and visual identity while
preserving the required source-code notices.

## Future ASBL Stewardship

topic.earth is intended to be stewarded by a Belgian ASBL. When the ASBL is
created, trademark and domain stewardship should be transferred or licensed to
that entity through written records.
