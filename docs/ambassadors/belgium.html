Support and feedback return to the communes

Purpose of this circle

Connect the regional and Community circles, organise multilingual exchanges and identify educational contacts. Support the participating communes without imposing one model, one language or one party position.

A first small step

Invite one interested contact from each of the four pilot circles to agree an accessible exchange format.

Independent accounting first

Before handling funds, the proposed ASBL must appoint an independent accountant, potentially from another region, with declared interests and no financial stake in the projects reviewed. Voluntary barter and mutual help are the pilot model. Funds are allowed only for documented basic operating costs: publish an agreed budget, receipts, reimbursements and an independently reviewed summary, while protecting personal data. No commercial investment or profit distribution through the pilot. This is a proposed governance rule, not a statement that an accountant or ASBL has already been appointed or registered.

A mandate from the participants

Propose one local Ambassador and a deputy per participating commune, chosen openly by its participating group. A defined term, accessible nominations, published decision rules, recall and appeal protect accountability. Participation does not claim to represent every resident. Wider delegates receive a limited mandate from participating circles and report back; they cannot overrule local choices.

Voluntary, organised meetings

The commune chooses private, network-only or public visibility. Publish goals and an agreed summary; share names, contacts, participant lists or precise meeting addresses only with consent. Identify an organiser, accessibility arrangements, conduct rules and a reporting contact. Future online meeting tools require permissions and moderation before launch.

Public interest, transparent resources

Declare interests and funding; a person cannot approve their own project alone. Publish reasons and corrections, allow independent review and an appeal. Donations cannot buy votes, ranking or a verified label. Keep topic.earth non-profit governance, resources and messaging separate from the SPRL and personal tutorial activities; confirm the ASBL legal status before fundraising.

Learn by doing

Start with a shared garden and fair harvest agreement, composting, repair and tool lending, local food or shared journeys. Invite educators to document practical skills and barriers. Measure participation, inclusion, repairs or useful journeys first; report environmental estimates with methods and limits.

Belgium: diversity without confusion

Belgium has three Regions: Brussels-Capital, Flanders and Wallonia, and three Communities: French-speaking, Flemish and German-speaking. The German-speaking Community is within Wallonia, not a fourth Region. The four pilot entries are cooperation circles, not a new administrative map. Brussels uses English here for international accessibility; French and Dutch remain essential for local participation.
