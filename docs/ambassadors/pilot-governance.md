# topic.earth — Ambassador pilot

Decision proposal for Ben's review · 12 October 2026.

## Start with people and one useful action

Use **Ambassadors**, rather than “Embassies”, for the public layer: it describes voluntary representatives without suggesting diplomatic or governmental authority. A civic cooperation circle may later use an “embassy” metaphor, with a clear explanation of its citizen status.

The numbered reading order is Anthisnes → Wallonia → German-speaking Community → Flanders → Brussels → Belgium → EU (local first, then peer regional/community circles, then country and supranational cooperation). This is an educational network sequence, not an administrative chain: Brussels, Flanders and Wallonia are peer Regions; the German-speaking Community is within Wallonia. The decision flow runs the other way: participants in a commune choose their action and delegate a limited mandate to wider circles. Advice, resources and feedback return to the communes. A later world circle connects peer country networks; it does not centralise control.

Belgium has **three Regions**, not four: Brussels-Capital, Flanders and Wallonia. It also has three Communities. The German-speaking Community, within Wallonia, has its own pilot entry. This network is not an administrative map. Brussels defaults to English in this educational example, with French and Dutch essential for participation; Flanders defaults to Dutch, Wallonia to French and the German-speaking Community to German. EU and Belgium default to English; Anthisnes to French.

Sources: [Belgium.be — Regions](https://www.belgium.be/en/about_belgium/government/regions), [Communities](https://www.belgium.be/en/about_belgium/government/communities). These sources establish geographic context, not a project endorsement.

## Proposed responsibility at each level

| Circle | Participants choose | Delegate helps with | Reports back to |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Commune | Action, organiser, one Ambassador and deputy, visibility | Listening, accessible meetings, local follow-up | Its participating group |
| Regional / Community | Voluntary intercommunal coordination | Connections, association exchanges, online meeting organisation | Participating communal circles |
| Country | Shared priorities across languages and circles | Regional support, educational contacts, exchanges with other countries | Participating regional / Community circles |
| EU | Agreed cross-country questions | Methods, learning opportunities, contacts and feedback | Participating country circles |

A participating group does not automatically represent every resident or the municipality. An official municipal mandate must be distinguished from a topic.earth volunteer mandate. Start small: one accountable local Ambassador plus a deputy, with an open route for new participants and dissent.

Before elections, agree eligibility, term length, accessible nominations, voting rules, representation across circles, conflicts of interest, recall, independent review and appeal. Do not present unimplemented voting or identity controls as operational. Do not weight influence by money, popularity, time available or AI use.

## Independent accounting first — règle proposée pour l’ASBL

Before managing funds, appoint an **independent accountant**, potentially from another region. Independence means declared interests and no financial stake in the projects reviewed; geography alone does not guarantee it. Publish a budget and independently reviewed financial summaries, protecting private donor and participant details. Confirm ASBL registration, accounting responsibilities and authorised financial signatories before collecting money.

**Avant de gérer des fonds, l’ASBL doit nommer un comptable indépendant**, éventuellement d’une autre région, sans intérêt financier dans les projets examinés. Budget, justificatifs, remboursements et synthèses contrôlées doivent rendre l’utilisation des moyens compréhensible. Cette règle est une proposition de gouvernance topic.earth, pas l’affirmation d’une obligation légale générale ou d’une ASBL déjà constituée.

The pilot permits voluntary barter, lending and mutual help. Funds cover only agreed, documented **basic operating costs**. No commercial investment, profit distribution or purchase of votes, visibility or verification. Reimbursements need an agreed policy, evidence and review. Keep topic.earth resources and decisions separate from CAD-AI-Support SPRL and the personal SmΔrt tutorial ecosystem.

## Meeting privacy belongs to the local group

Choose private, network-only or public visibility for each meeting and its records. Publish a useful approved summary without automatically publishing attendee identities or precise addresses. Sharing contacts requires consent. Identify an organiser, accessibility provisions, conduct rules, safeguarding and a reporting contact before inviting people.

Choose a first feasible activity: a garden with a fair harvest agreement, compost, repair and tool lending, local food or shared journeys. Share practical outcomes and difficulties; technical energy projects need qualified feasibility work. AI and coding are optional.

## Current delivery and next implementation

These seven examples and diagrams are public static educational content. Existing proposals are browser-local drafts; export and agreed sharing remain necessary. The site does not yet provide verified membership, elections, protected meeting rooms, accounting controls or booking through this content.

Static examples add no external service dependency; existing hosting charges remain. Private collaboration and democratic voting are a separate project of greater complexity: server-side permissions, verification, moderation, audit records, consent and accountable administration. Agree the governance with a small pilot group before choosing providers or estimating a service budget.

Next: discuss the rules with interested participants and an independent accountant, confirm the ASBL status, agree a charter, run one consent-based local meeting, document its outcome and revise before expansion. No named municipality, person, association or EU body is claimed as a confirmed partner.
