# Pluropoly / Monoploy Private Confession

Status: private concept note, not public release copy.
Date: 2026-05-22

This note records a dream that is connected to topic.earth, but is not ready to be posted as a finished promise.

## Monoploy

Monoploy is intentional. It is not Monopoly. It is the failing Mars-colonisation game: cars, FPV piloting, people to reach, points to score, and a deliberately uneasy scenario about escape fantasies, extraction, and bad control. It can appear around Mars as a symbolic and playable branch, but it should not be treated as the main public mission of topic.earth today.

The hidden-Musk / Mars / Monoploy idea is a narrative device: a way to show how a game can be exciting and still point toward a bad civilisational instinct if the goal is only domination, ownership, or escape.

## Pluropoly

Pluropoly is the future masterpiece dream. It is a remake/answer to Monoploy, but based on the Fever loop.

The core idea: users, and possibly AI players, move through a game system where every action changes the future scenario. The goal is not to become richest, but to reach the best viable climate and social scenario over the next 100 years.

This is grounded in the topic.earth Fever loop: choices should be judged by whether they help humanity move toward a sustainable path. If moves are not sustainable, the player does not simply lose money; the world state drifts toward worse futures.

The best scenario is presented as the only available path for humankind in the next century, unless extraordinary scientific progress gives humanity much stronger control of the thermal world. The game should make that pressure understandable, playable, and emotionally clear.

## Relationship To Topic.Earth

For now, the real-world project is topic.earth.

topic.earth should stay focused on:

- helping people understand climate systems, AI, regional action, and global risk;
- allowing users to create and share useful HTML/topic releases;
- making educational material that schools, communities, and curious people can use;
- building trust before expanding into bigger game worlds.

Pluropoly can grow from topic.earth later, in bigger and bigger parts, once the foundation is strong enough and the idea can be shown responsibly.

## Personal Note

This is a confession to a trusted collaborator, not a pitch deck.

The dream is large, emotional, and not yet ready for the world. Keep it protected. Let topic.earth carry the first public work: clear education, beautiful topic releases, and a place where humans and AI can help explain the climate and AI century together.
