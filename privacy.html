Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 22, 2026

topic.earth is a static-first Earth intelligence dashboard. The app is designed to work locally in your browser, with user-controlled AI features that are only used when enabled or triggered.

Information We Store Locally

topic.earth may store settings, language preferences, local drafts, display choices, and user-selected API configuration in your browser storage. This information stays on your device unless you intentionally export it, submit it, or use a connected service.

AI Features

Some features can use OpenAI or another AI provider for translation, reading, summarizing, or topic drafting. When you use those features, the selected text or prompt may be sent to the configured AI provider to generate a response. Do not enter private, sensitive, or confidential information unless you understand the provider's terms and privacy policy.

API Keys

For local/admin testing, API keys may be entered by the user and stored in the browser. Public deployments should use a backend bridge instead of exposing secrets in frontend code. Never publish API keys in screenshots, source files, URLs, or shared logs.

Cookies And Tracking

topic.earth does not use advertising trackers by default. If analytics, cookies, or external tracking services are added later, this policy should be updated before they are enabled for public users.

Third-Party Services

The app may load assets or data from services such as Cloudinary, OpenStreetMap-related providers, Vercel, GitHub, and AI providers when a feature needs them. Those services may process technical data such as IP address, browser details, request logs, or submitted content according to their own policies.

Children

topic.earth is educational, but it is not intended to collect personal information from children. Children should use AI and contribution features with adult guidance.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, contact support@topic.earth.
